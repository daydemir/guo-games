# Guo Games
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User preference: Vite + React + TypeScript, lean dependencies, localStorage.
## Users
Kevin, Deniz, Nick, Jack, Simon, Nate, Dmitriy at Kevin's Maui bachelor party. Deniz and Nick organize. Spectators welcome.
## Product Purpose
A shared attention engine: a few predictions, one thoughtful act, phone away, quiet witness confirmation, dinner reveal, time capsule.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Standalone local-only installable PWA. No backend, MIX services, credentials, real money, risky prompts, or compulsory participation. Local identities are a demonstration, not authentication. Private content is hidden in the interface, not encrypted.
## Brand Commitments
Restrained Maui-night and trading-card visual flavor. No gradients, em dashes, fake testimonials, or glassmorphism.
## Evidence on Hand
User brief and The Guo Games — Ideas & Product Direction.docx, read without modifying source.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
390px and desktop, semantic keyboard controls, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, spectator participation.
## Delegated Defaults
User explicitly says not to block on unanswered product questions. Default story awards and rankings hidden until dinner. Dates are configurable demo assumptions, not confirmed trip dates.
